FAQ

Questions contractors ask before estimating with Vestimate.

Everything you need to know about tracing, estimating, quantities, workflows, and construction takeoffs.

Can Vestimate trace directly from PDF plans?
Yes. Vestimate is designed for tracing directly from PDF construction drawings, allowing you to generate accurate quantities faster without redrawing plans manually.
Does Vestimate support DXF or AutoCAD files?
Yes. Vestimate supports DXF workflows in Pro so you can work with CAD-based drawings, AutoCAD exports, and hybrid estimating processes.
Do I have to choose between tracing and typing dimensions?
No. You can mix PDF tracing, DXF imports, and manual dimension input within the same estimate. All workflows feed into the same BOM and estimating process.
Can I use Vestimate offline?
Yes. Core estimating, PDF takeoff, local storage, and PDF export can work offline. AI features, license activation, live rates, and first-time XLSX library loading require internet.
Can Vestimate help with government or BOQ projects?
Yes. Vestimate supports quantity workflows commonly used in BOQ preparation, government bidding, and construction documentation including DPWH-style estimates.
Is Vestimate accurate enough for professional estimating?
Vestimate is designed to help contractors and estimators speed up quantity takeoff workflows while maintaining professional-level accuracy and consistency.
Who is Vestimate for?
Vestimate is built for contractors, estimators, quantity surveyors, architects, engineers, and construction teams who want faster and more efficient takeoff workflows.
Can I use Vestimate for Philippine construction estimates?
Yes. Vestimate is designed around practical construction workflows commonly used in the Philippines, including local estimating practices and BOQ preparation.
Does Vestimate replace Excel?
No. Vestimate works alongside your existing Excel workflows. It helps speed up quantity tracing and scope generation while still fitting into familiar spreadsheet-based estimating processes.
